# Interviews

## Person 1

**Gender:** Male
**Age:** 17
**Occupation:** Student / car mechanic trainee
**Household:** Lives with his mom and sister
**Tech Literacy:** Medium

**How often do you cook?**
Almost every day. I like to make my own food, so I can follow my diet plan.

**What is important to you when you plan for a meal?**
That it is healthy, have a lot of protein, and taste good

**How often do you try out new recipes?**
Maybe twice a month

**Where do you find new recipes?**
Sometimes I see them on TikTok and want to try them out. Or sometimes I search for recipes with specific ingredients and decide what to try out based on the search result.

**What is most important to you to when you choose which recipe to try out? (E.g., easy to understand, simple ingredients, experience from others.)**
I like simple step-by-step recipes that are easy to follow. And recipes that don’t take too long to cook.

**Is there something you dislike with the websites/apps you’ve used when cooking?**
If there is a lot of unnecessary text, I often skip the recipe and move on to another website. I have dyslexia and don’t want to deal with reading through a lot of text that I don’t need to. And if there is a lot of ads in between the recipe – that makes it so confusing.

**Is there something you like with the websites/apps you’ve used when cooking?**
I like it when you can make the screen stay on, so you don’t have to touch the screen every other minute.

## Person 2

**Gender:** Female
**Age:** 26
**Occupation:** Educational leader in kindergarten
**Household:** Lives with her partner
**Tech Literacy:** Medium

**How often do you cook?**
Every day with a few exceptions. We eat out or order takeaway maybe twice a month.

**What is important to you when you plan for a meal?**
My partner is a bit picky, so it must be approved by him. Otherwise, it is nice that it is healthy and take a short amount of time to cook.

**How often do you try out new recipes?**
I love to try out new things, but I don’t do it as much as I want to. So maybe 1-2 times a month.

**Where do you find new recipes?**
If I need a recipe for something specific, I use Google. But if I need new ideas, I use websites like Matprat.no, Godt.no, or the grocery store’s websites.

**What is most important to you to when you choose which recipe to try out? (E.g., easy to understand, simple ingredients, experience from others.)**
That it is easy to understand, and easy access to ingredients (I skip if I need to get ingredients from special stores).

**Is there something you dislike with the websites/apps you’ve used when cooking?**
When there is no filtering, or when filtering functions don’t work. If you search for pasta, and dishes without pasta is showing.

**Is there something you like with the websites/apps you’ve used when cooking?**
I like it when the website has clear sorting options based on time, difficultness etc.

## Person 4

**Gender:** Male
**Age:** 49
**Occupation:** Truck driver / Psyche ward assistant (every other week)
**Household:** Lives with his daughter (20y)
**Tech Literacy:** High

**How often do you cook?**
The weeks I am in my truck I obviously don’t cook. But the weeks I am at home I try to cook maybe 3-4 times.

**What is important to you when you plan for a meal?**
I am a food lover, so when I plan for the week I am home, I want some tasteful meals with variety of meats, sides and vegetables.

**How often do you try out new recipes?**
Maybe once a month. If I have some free time.

**Where do you find new recipes?**
Sometimes recipes are showing up in my feed in social media. If they look good, I save them for later. I also follow some food bloggers that have some interesting recipes.

**What is most important to you to when you choose which recipe to try out? (E.g., easy to understand, simple ingredients, experience from others.)**
It needs to be something more than just basics. I like it when I can challenge myself in cooking, and I don’t mind if it takes a lot of time.

**Is there something you dislike with the websites/apps you’ve used when cooking?**
I have stopped using blogs and websites that has overwhelming ads. And I don’t like it if you must log in to see recipe details.

**Is there something you like with the websites/apps you’ve used when cooking?**
I like the websites that let me save recipes for later, so I don’t have to search for the same recipe multiple times.

## Person 5

**Gender:** Female
**Age:** 70
**Occupation:** Pensioner
**Household:** Lives with her partner
**Tech Literacy:** Medium

**How often do you cook?**
Almost every day

**What is important to you when you plan for a meal?**
I follow a strict gluten-free, lactose-free, low FOD-map diet plan. It is important that recipes follow this or can be adapted into this.

**How often do you try out new recipes?**
Maybe once a month.

**Where do you find new recipes?**
Mostly in gluten-free groups where we share recipes and experiences. If I am looking for something special, I google it and visit the websites that seems best then and there.

**What is most important to you to when you choose which recipe to try out? (E.g., easy to understand, simple ingredients, experience from others.)**
Based on the food restrictions I have I like to hear experience from other with the same restrictions.

**Is there something you dislike with the websites/apps you’ve used when cooking?**
I hate it when the ads take over half of the page and are impossible to close. Too many ads and pop-ups, bad layouts, or websites with bad structures make me give up and find other websites.

**Is there something you like with the websites/apps you’ve used when cooking?**
I like pages that are easy and logical to move through, with easy filtering and sorting. If there are comments available, I like to read through them to see what others have experienced making the recipe.
