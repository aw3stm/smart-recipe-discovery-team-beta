# Persona 1

### Demographic & Professional Overview

**Gender:** Female
**Age:** 35
**Occupation:** Producer of Visual communication
**Household:** Lives with her partner and two kids (2y and 3y)
**Tech Literacy:** Medium
**Cooking Frequency:** 6-7 days a week

### Bio & Background

This person works full-time with visual communication, producing signs, posters etc in large scales. Her workday is busy and she is always on her feet. Since her partner works night shifts, she gets most of the afternoons alone with the kids.

After work she have a long commute and she needs to pick up the children from kindergarden. When they get home there is very little time in the afternoon to plan for dinner. She still want the childeren to have nice, homecooked meals, so it is often the same familiar dinners they make.

When she looks for new recipes, she often uses Snapchat, on her phone. She likes that you can follow a step-by-step video, but hates that the ads disrupts in the middle of a video.

### Goals & Motivations

• **Kid Friendly:** Search for recipes that are simple, with kid-friendly ingredients.
• **Short cooking time:** Find recipes that are quick to make.
• **Easy Cooking:** Follow clear, step-by-step instructions without disruptive ads.

### Pain Points & Frustrations

• **Intrusive ads:** Disruptive ads in the middle of videos
• **Complicated recipes:** Recipes with lots of ingredients and half a day of cooking doesn’t match the family with small children.
• **Bad layout/UX:** Hard to sort out and filter what you are looking for. Bad layout that makes it hard to find what you are looking for.

### Devices and Habits

• **Primary Device:** Uses mobile phone only
• **Key Browsing Behaviour:** Expects easy step-by-step recipes without disruptive ads, and easy search/filter access.

## Implications for the Product Team

**Persona Need**
• Needs easy search and filter options
• Wants recipes to be easy to read and follow

**Feature / Technical Requirement**
• Implement debounced search input and instant category filtering.
• Display clear UI badge pills with tags on the recipe cards.
• Ensure clean responsive layout with no intrusive ads

# Persona 2

### Demographic & Professional Overview

**Gender:** Male
**Age:** 49
**Occupation:** Truck driver / Psyche ward assistant (every other week)
**Household:** Lives with his daughter (20y)
**Tech Literacy:** High
**Cooking Frequency:** 3-4 days a week

### Bio & Background

This person works as a long distance truck driver with one week on/one week off turnus. In the weeks he works, he sleeps in the truck and eats either pre-cooked meals from home or at gas stations. In his weeks off he works part-time as a psyche ward assistant. He loves to cook, so in his weeks off he prioritise cooking when he has time. He like to challenge himself with special recipes and is not afraid to spend hours in the kitchen.

He often saves recipes that shows up in social media, and follows food bloggers that he finds interesting. He hates blogs and websites with overwhelming ads, and don’t like that you must log in to accounts to see recipes. He think it’s great when you can save your favourite recipe for later.

### Goals & Motivations

• **Interesting recipes:** Search for recipes that are something extra.
• **Filtering based on ingredients:** Find recipes that match the ingredients he wants to use.
• **Saving recipes:** Be able to save recipes as favourites without logging in

### Pain Points & Frustrations

• **Intrusive ads:** Overwhelming ads that take up half the screen.
• **Log in for basic things:** Logging in to view or save recipes.
• **Bad layout/UX:** Lack of responsive design, especially in mobile versions.

### Devices and Habits

• **Devices:** Mobile and Ipad
• **Key Browsing Behaviour:** Expects a responsive design that looks the same on mobile as on Ipad, good filtering options based on ingredients, and a possibility to save recipes for later.

## Implications for the Product Team

**Persona Need**
• Needs easy search and filter options
• Wants a design that looks the same on phone as on Ipad
• Want to save recipes without creating an account

**Feature / Technical Requirement**
• Implement debounced search input and instant category filtering.
• Ensure a good responsive layout that works perfect on all devices.
• Build a client-side “Favourites” system persisted via localStorage
